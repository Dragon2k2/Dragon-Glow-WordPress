## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a transcendent, clinical-luxury aesthetic for an avant-garde 2026 skincare brand. It fuses high-science dermatological efficacy with ethereal, ritualistic indulgence. The visual voice is luminous, serene, and exquisitely tactile—evoking the sensory tactility of pearlescent emulsions, warm botanical oils, and radiant morning sunlight playing against alabaster stone.

The target audience encompasses discerning luxury consumers seeking prestige self-care rituals backed by visible performance. Interfaces must communicate effortless opulence without ostentation. Every touchpoint feels bespoke, air-lit, and polished.

Drawing from an intersection of **Warm Minimalism** and **Refractive Glassmorphism**, the interface relies on vast, breathable negative space, delicate golden hairline framing, light-scattering frosted planes, and soft multi-spectrum ambient halos. The overall experience feels weightless, glowing from within rather than reflecting external glare.

## Colors

The palette captures pure photonic radiance, soft botanical heat, and grounding mineral depth:

- **Primary Glow (`#D4AF37` - Radiant Golden Amber):** Represents solar warmth, bio-fermented golden active ingredients, and gilded micro-accents. Used sparingly for high-prestige focal points, primary actions, and fine linear embellishments.
- **Secondary Heat (`#FF7556` - Sunset Dragon Fruit):** A vibrant, high-energy biological coral used to highlight immediate clinical results, active skin metrics, dynamic offers, and high-impact micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary Softness (`#F9A88F` - Dragon Fruit Blush):** A gentle, pearlescent peach tone designed for luminous badge fills, gradient underlays, ambient glow orbs, and soft status indicators.
- **Neutral Deep (`#1A1A1A` - Deep Mineral Charcoal):** Grounding carbon tone that provides razor-sharp editorial legibility against alabaster surfaces without the clinical harshness of pitch black.
- **Surface Foundations:** 
  - Canvas Base: `#FDFCF7` (Luminous Alabaster Silk)
  - Nested Surface: `#FAF8F5` (Warm Cream Porcelain)
  - Elevated Plate: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Radiant Pearlescence at variable transparencies from 70% to 92%)
  - Secondary Text: `#5C5852` (Warm Slate)
  - Hairline Border: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)` (Spun Gold Trace)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances haute-parfumerie elegance with clinical legibility. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Unfurls brand identity, ritual narratives, and hero declarations. High contrast stroke widths convey classic prestige, softened by gentle tracking. It is never used in all-caps for paragraphs.
- **Body & Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers ingredient breakdowns, clinical efficacy percentages, and product descriptions with clean, modern clarity. Its rounded terminals echo the soft organic geometry of skincare droppers and droplets.
- **Editorial Micro-Labels:** All `label-*` tokens must be rendered in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.1em` to `0.14em`) to confer editorial curation, batch numbers, and scientific certifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts an editorial fluid grid punctuated by generous vertical pauses that mimic the cadence of a curated high-fashion publication.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout, `margin-lg` (4rem / 64px) outer boundaries, `gutter-lg` (2.5rem / 40px) between columns. Maximum container width capped at 1440px to prevent visual dilution of curated layouts.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, `margin` (2rem / 32px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px).
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-sm` (1.25rem / 20px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px).
- **Rhythm & Negative Space:** Modules are separated by expansive vertical gaps (minimum 64px on mobile, up to 128px on desktop) to cultivate an aura of calm, unhurried discovery. Asymmetrical offsets are intentionally utilized in feature collections to showcase product vessels as sculptural art pieces.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through optical light transmission rather than heavy opaque dropping. Elements float as if suspended in crystalline fluid:

- **Level 1 (Subtle Vessel):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.02)`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`. Used for passive interactive cards and utility trays.
- **Level 2 (Luminous Float):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(255, 117, 86, 0.08), 0 6px 16px -2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`. Used for hovered cards, active drawers, and flyout menus.
- **Level 3 (Ritual Focus Modal):** `0 24px 64px -8px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
- **Pearlescent Glass Standard:** Surfaces leverage `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)` atop `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`. All glass surfaces feature an internal pseudo-border inset with a radial gradient simulating light refractance along polished edges (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.9), rgba(212,175,55,0.25) 50%, rgba(249,168,143,0.15))`).

## Shapes

The design system employs Level 2 (Rounded) curvature, honoring the organic contours of skincare stones, smooth water-worn pebbles, and rounded glass flacons.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to micro-components such as data tags, clinical indicator pills, and form input frames.
- **Medium Radius (1rem / 16px):** Standard for product cards, modal containers, and checkout summaries.
- **Large Radius (1.5rem / 24px):** Utilized for hero banners, floating cart panels, and focal feature viewports.
- **Pill (9999px):** Strictly reserved for primary CTA buttons, editorial floating category pills, and promotional badges to convey tactile softness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Glowing Action:** Pill-shaped, gradient fill transitioning from `#D4AF37` to `#FF7556` via `#E6B800`. Typography is `label-lg` in crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle text shadow. Box-shadow produces an ambient halo: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(255, 117, 86, 0.35)`. On hover, the halo expands and shifts slightly golden.
- **Secondary Glass Action:** Pill-shaped, transparent with a 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`. Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` with backdrop blur. Text in `#1A1A1A`. Hover triggers background saturation to pure pearlescent white.
- **Text Link:** Editorial serif or uppercase sans accompanied by a hairline animated underline that sweeps in from left to right using a champagne gold fill.

### Cards & Product Displays
- **Skin-Glow Finish:** Product containers utilize an off-white silk base (`#FFFFFF` over `#FAF8F5`) layered with an ultra-soft radial inner vignette tinted with Sunset Dragon Fruit (`rgba(255, 117, 86, 0.03)`).
- **Artwork Frame:** Imagery sits inside seamless, borderless frames that allow bottle renders to bleed organically across the container space.
- **Quick-Add Overlay:** A floating frosted glass bar emerges at the base of the card upon hover, featuring a seamless split between direct cart addition and shade selection.

### Badges & Clinical Proof Chips
- **Proof Badges:** Compact pills with `label-sm` tracking. Background is `rgba(249, 168, 143, 0.15)` framed by a micro-border of `rgba(255, 117, 86, 0.3)`. Text is styled in deep coral charcoal (`#9E3B27`).
- **Purity & Formulation Badges:** Circular or soft squircle icons cast in spun-gold wireframe (`#D4AF37`) accompanied by ultra-clean clinical percentages (e.g., "99.4% Luminous Recovery").

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Floating underline or pill-boxed field with `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` border and pearlescent backdrop. Focused states illuminate the entire inner container with a warm, ambient gold aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular custom controls. When active, they reveal an inner glowing orb resembling an amber botanical oil droplet.

### Navigation & Headers
- **Minimalist Suspended Ribbon:** Fixed navbar floats 16px below the top viewport boundary, utilizing the Pearlescent Glass standard with pill-shaped boundaries, housing razor-thin serif typography, a centered gilded insignia, and an ethereal drawer-based micro-bag counter.
