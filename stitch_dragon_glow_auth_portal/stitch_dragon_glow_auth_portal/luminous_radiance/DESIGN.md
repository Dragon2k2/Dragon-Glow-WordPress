## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of modern prestige cosmetics: golden hour luminescence, mindful skincare rituals, and quiet opulence. Built for the discerning beauty consumer, the visual atmosphere balances serene cream landscapes with radiant golden warmth and botanical precision.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Luminous Glassmorphism**:
- Soft, ambient radial gradients evoke golden hour sunlight diffused through frosted glass.
- High-end cosmetic product photography takes center stage within organic, smoothly rounded contours.
- Restrained visual hierarchy ensures an unhurried, ritualistic shopping journey reminiscent of high-touch luxury apothecary flagships.
- Interactions are frictionless, deliberate, and weightless, projecting clinical efficacy enveloped in tactile elegance.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from sunlight, raw silk, and metallic amber pigments, grounded by a rich roasted espresso neutral.

- **Primary (`#8C6D23`)**: Deep antique gold used for brand authority, editorial titles, active navigation states, and primary iconography.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27`)**: Luminous molten gold dedicated to primary calls to action, badges, and high-engagement touchpoints.
- **Tertiary (`#E8D9B5`)**: Champagne silk used for subtle tinted containers, pill tags, and soft border strokes.
- **Neutral (`#1F1C18`)**: Deep warm espresso charcoal providing high-contrast readability across headlines and body copy without the harshness of pure black.

### Canvas & Surface Tokens
- **Canvas Base**: `#FDFBF7` (porcelain cream).
- **Surface Muted**: `#F7F3EB` (sunlit sand).
- **Glass Panel Surface**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` layered over subtle gold ambient blurs.
- **Border Subtle**: `rgba(140, 109, 35, 0.12)`.

## Typography

The type system balances the classical elegance of **Playfair Display** with the geometric warmth and crisp legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Headings**: Set in Playfair Display with tightened letter tracking and organic leading, creating an editorial presence that matches luxury physical beauty print publications.
- **Eyebrows & Section Markers**: Rendered in uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans at small sizes with wide letter spacing (`0.12em`) to structure content clearly.
- **Body & Product Metadata**: Handled by Plus Jakarta Sans to deliver frictionless shopping utility, rapid scanning of ingredients, and accessible mobile reading.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an airy, breathable 12-column responsive fluid grid designed around ample white space to impart luxury exclusivity:

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12-column grid with `margin-desktop` (64px) and `gutter-lg` (32px). Maximum container width is constrained to `1320px` to maintain focused visual cohesion.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid with `margin` (32px) and `gutter` (24px).
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid with `margin-mobile` (20px) and `gutter-sm` (16px).

Section vertical pacing uses `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` gaps, allowing editorial storytelling, curated rituals, and visual showcases breathing room. Horizontal carousels snap along grid boundaries with slight preview bleeds to encourage gestural exploration.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold, artificial shadows, relying instead on warm diffused ambient light and translucent glass layers.

- **Level 1 (Subtle Floats & Cards)**: `0 4px 20px rgba(140, 109, 35, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(31, 28, 24, 0.04)`. Used for product display cards and testimonial surfaces.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Popovers)**: `0 12px 32px rgba(140, 109, 35, 0.12), 0 2px 6px rgba(31, 28, 24, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Nav & Modals)**: `0 20px 48px rgba(31, 28, 24, 0.08), 0 4px 12px rgba(140, 109, 35, 0.08)`.
- **Luminous Radiance Effect**: Background blur elements use layered CSS filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` to `blur(24px)`) over linear ambient washes (`radial-gradient(circle, rgba(232, 217, 181, 0.35) 0%, rgba(253, 251, 247, 0) 70%)`).
- **Surface Outlines**: Elevated cards are framed with an ultra-fine `1px` stroke tinted in `rgba(140, 109, 35, 0.12)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` on light backgrounds to simulate beveled perfume flacons.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** shape language (`roundedness: 2`, base radius `0.5rem` / `8px`), evoking organic botanical symmetry and ergonomic cosmetic bottles.

- **Standard Cards & Surfaces**: Built with `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to present soft, welcoming silhouettes across product frames, philosophy spotlights, and review tiles.
- **Buttons & Interactive Tags**: Styled with full pill rounding (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for touch targets, chips, category filters, and navigation bubbles.
- **Input Fields & Search Bars**: Retain soft `0.75rem` (`12px`) geometry to maintain structured balance alongside rounded imagery.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), warm amber-gold gradient background (`linear-gradient(135deg, #DBB13B 0%, #C59B27 100%)`), espresso text (`#1F1C18`), font weight 600. Subtle luminous glow on hover (`0 6px 20px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: Pill-shaped, transparent background, `1px` solid border (`#8C6D23` at 30% opacity), text in `#1F1C18`. Shifts to delicate golden fill (`rgba(232, 217, 181, 0.2)`) on hover.
- **Icon Buttons (Bag, Wishlist, Navigation Arrows)**: Circular (`44px × 44px`), soft gold tinted background (`#E8D9B5` at 80% opacity or white glassmorphism), subtle scale transition (`transform: scale(1.04)`) on hover.

### Cards
- **Product Card**: Clean sand-toned canvas (`#FFFFFF` or `#F7F3EB`), `24px` corner radii, `1px` hairline border in `rgba(140, 109, 35, 0.1)`. Media block nested with `16px` inner margin, featuring gentle image zoom on card hover. Price and title cleanly typeset with direct "Quick Add" trigger.
- **Curated Ritual Card**: Tall aspect ratio (3:4) with background imagery completely framed by rounded corners, featuring floating bottom glass-morphism category labels and category titles set in high-contrast white Playfair Display over soft bottom vignettes.
- **Review Card**: Glassmorphic frosted panel (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75); backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), framed with soft gold star ratings, italicized quotation body, and circular customer avatar with verified gold check.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Micro Badge (Floating on Images)**: Small pill-shaped tag, frosted white-gold surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), deep espresso text, padding `4px 12px`, uppercase tracking.
- **Category Filter**: Pill-shaped, borderless sand surface (`#F7F3EB`) in rest state; converts to warm gold with white text when selected.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Checkout Fields**: Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(140, 109, 35, 0.2)`, `12px` border radius. Focus state reveals a gentle amber focus ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.2)`) and shifts border to `#C59B27`. Floating placeholder labels set in neutral muted tone.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls**: Custom circular radios and rounded square checkboxes (`4px` radius) with `1.5px` border in `#8C6D23`. Active state displays solid gold fill (`#C59B27`) with crisp white check/pip icon.

### Commerce Enhancements
- **Sticky Header**: Translucent frosted cream bar (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), slim bottom border `rgba(140, 109, 35, 0.08)`. Features gold-accented cart count badge.
- **Star Rating Indicator**: 5-star metric colored in polished warm gold (`#C59B27`) accompanied by fractional score in body-sm typography.
